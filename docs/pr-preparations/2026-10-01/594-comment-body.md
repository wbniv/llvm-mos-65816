Hi @mlund, thanks for laying this foundation. I'm preparing 65816 far-data support for upstream ([#320 series](https://github.com/wbniv/llvm-mos-65816/blob/main/docs/pr-preparations/2026-09-30/split-320-321/REVIEWER-MAP-320.md)), and it needs the same 32-bit imaginary register family as this PR. Rather than send a competing copy, I've rebuilt the series on top of #594: your three commits come first, unchanged, with your authorship. I'd like to agree on a few points with you before either of us files.

1. **Land #594 as the shared foundation.** The far series then only adds to it. The one change I had to make when rebasing onto current upstream is a single token: `MOSImagReg32` takes `bits<16> num`, but since #571 MOS register numbers are 32 bits wide, so it needs `bits<32>`.

2. **A crash with stack arguments.** Applied alone to current upstream (`06bc967d2668`), #594 makes any function whose call passes stack arguments abort in the register coalescer with "Invalid global physical register", in every MOS CPU mode. A four-line variadic `main` is enough. The cause: LLVM treats a register unit as reserved only when all of its super-registers are reserved, and `RL` isn't reserved over the reserved `RS` pair, so the soft stack pointer's bytes stop counting as reserved. The series fixes it in the commit right after yours by reserving a quad whenever either of its pairs is reserved. I'm happy for you to take that into #594 itself.

3. **Allocation and your contract.** The far series needs `RL` to be allocatable, because it passes far pointers in `RL1`–`RL3`. For now it enables allocation only on the 65816, behind one predicate (`hasAllocatableImag32()`); other CPUs get an empty allocation order, so their code is unchanged. I'd rather switch that predicate to the SDK contract from your `imag32-contract` branch, and supply the declaration for the SNES platform, whose linker script we control and can guarantee contiguous four-byte imaginary registers. Would that work for you, or would you rather land the allocation step yourself?

4. **DWARF numbering.** On current upstream, `Imag16RegsOffset + MaxImag16Regs` gives `RL` numbers `0x30080 + K`. Under #571's scheme that falls inside the `RS` type `0x03` range, and the lldb MOS plugin knows only the `RC` and `RS` banks. Should `RL` get its own type bank in the MOS DWARF specification? The series currently keeps your formula, and switching is a one-line change.

5. **One far address space.** The series uses `addrspace(2)` for far data: 32-bit pointers holding a 24-bit bank address, lowered to 65816 long addressing (`[dp]`, `[dp],Y`, long absolute). Since MEGA65 `[ptr],z` is on your list, it would be good to settle on one far address space that each target lowers its own way, before either lands.

Does that direction work for you (and @mysterymath)? The far support already drives a published SNES demo, the [LZSS gallery](https://biohack.net/snes/lzss-gallery/), which keeps its data in far memory.
