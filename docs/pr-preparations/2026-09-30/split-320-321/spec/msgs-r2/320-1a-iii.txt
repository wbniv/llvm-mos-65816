[MOS] Add nonallocatable 32-bit imaginary registers

Model each RL as two RS subregisters and support physical copies and
symbol lowering. Group RL aliases during zero-page CSR allocation.

Keep Imag32 nonallocatable because linker scripts may place adjacent RS
registers apart.
