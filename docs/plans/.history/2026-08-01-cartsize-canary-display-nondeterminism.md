Pinning the seed collapses\nall 24 boots to one hash (11D41DC5, #00FF28 -- the green verdict). Same result\non hello.c, 25 lines and known good: 6 distinct pictures in 8 runs, one when\npinned.\n\nThat withdraws the "jgxcheck captures stale/partial frames" finding recorded in\nthis file and in TODO.md.
