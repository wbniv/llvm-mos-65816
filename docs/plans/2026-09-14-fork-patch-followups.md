## Patch-stack conventions that govern this work

From [`agent-handoff.md`](../agent-handoff.md) and `dev/toolchain.sh` / `dev/regen-patch.sh`:

- `0002` is a full rsync mirror of `llvm/lib/Target/MOS/` regenerated **only** by `dev/regen-patch.sh`.
- Upstream-bound standalone fixes that live inside the MOS dir (`0003`, `0018`–`0025`) are applied by
  `dev/toolchain.sh` **after** `0002` and listed in `regen-patch.sh` (`P3` / `STANDALONE_MOSDIR`) so a
  `0002` regen reverse-applies them out and never absorbs them.
- The vendor "pin" is not a SHA in a file: `dev/toolchain.sh` does `git clone --depth 1` of upstream
  `main` and the checkout HEAD (`8be0546128a5`, the #563 merge) is what every patch applies on. A bump =
  re-clone at a newer upstream commit + re-verify the whole stack applies (see §0021 below).
