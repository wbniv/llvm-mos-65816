Rules

Tiles (5): TRACK_NEEDLE, TRACK_UNDER_HOT/MID/DIM, unchanged TRACK_LITERAL — five replace the zipper's four.

OAM: 16 entries owned; max scene 14 dashes + 1 needle. All 8×8, X 0..255, hidden at Y=240, whole shadow rebuilt per presentation, one VBlank DMA.

Cadence (unchanged, #129): hooks not frames — reveal = phase×2+1, clamp 15, 9th hook may latch the next match.

Fade is positional: age is computed from sample index each presentation, never accumulated — a cancelled or re-latched sweep cannot inherit stale fade.

Underline sits one row below the path so the needle crosses it rather than overlapping: that is the "under" in underline, and it keeps the two marks readable as separate things.
